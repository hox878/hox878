Who am I?

Hello! My name is Jiahong Wu . I graduated from the Department of Vehicle Engineering at National Formosa University and currently work as a Mechanical Engineer at Yamaha Motor R&D Taiwan (YMRT).

Outside of work, you'll probably find me gaming, reading manga, traveling, or taking photos along the way.

Why does this website exist?

My interest in programming started in my first year of university, when I studied Electrical Engineering at National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology. Programming quickly became my favorite subject, and even after I moved to Vehicle Engineering, I kept learning programming in my spare time.

Now, after work, I am teaching myself computer science through Harvard's CS50x, where I have learned C, Python, SQL, and some web development. This website is where I share what I learn and build.
